About Me

Hello, I’m Akram Mukasa, a Senior Software Quality Assurance Engineer with over 5 years of experience in ensuring the delivery of high-quality software solutions. My journey in technology has been driven by a passion for creating seamless user experiences and delivering robust, scalable, and reliable applications. I specialize in both manual and automated testing for web and mobile applications, leveraging tools and frameworks such as Selenium, Cypress, Playwright, Appium, and Cucumber BDD. My expertise extends to API testing using Postman and Python Rest Assured, as well as performance testing with tools like JMeter and Locust. Throughout my career, I’ve worked with diverse teams to design and implement comprehensive testing strategies, ensuring applications meet and exceed user expectations. My technical background includes proficiency in Python, JavaScript (React, Angular, and Vue.js), Java, AWS, and CI/CD pipelines, enabling me to collaborate effectively with development teams and contribute to the software development lifecycle. As a QA engineer, I take pride in identifying potential risks early, streamlining testing processes, and continuously enhancing product quality. I’m also deeply committed to innovation, exploring how emerging technologies like AI can be integrated into testing workflows for better efficiency and accuracy. When I’m not ensuring software quality, I enjoy mentoring aspiring tech professionals, sharing knowledge, and contributing to impactful projects. I’m passionate about fostering a culture of excellence and collaboration in every team I work with. Let’s connect and build software that makes a difference
